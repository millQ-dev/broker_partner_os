# Broker Partner OS

Decision operating system for building capital through disciplined investing.

## Operating model

- Long-biased by default.
- Shorts are allowed only as explicitly approved speculative trades.
- No automatic trade execution in the MVP.
- Every investment decision must record thesis, catalyst, horizon, entry, target, invalidation, risk and exit reason.
- Signal Intelligence may track rumors and early signals, but questionable non-public information is observation-only until lawful public confirmation.

## MVP modules

1. Portfolio — cash, accounts, positions, transactions, P&L, allocation.
2. Investment Ideas — lifecycle from watch to exit.
3. Market Data — quotes, fundamentals, events and news inputs.
4. Research Engine — structured thesis updates and catalyst tracking.
5. Risk Engine — sizing, max loss, concentration and thesis-break checks.
6. Decision Desk — BUY / WATCH / HOLD / REDUCE / EXIT / SPECULATIVE SHORT.
7. Signal Intelligence — source scoring and prediction history with a compliance gate.
