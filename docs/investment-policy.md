# Investment Policy

## Baseline
Broker Partner OS is long-biased. The primary objective is compounding capital over months and years, not maximizing trade frequency.

## Long positions
Every proposed long position must include:
- thesis;
- catalyst(s);
- horizon;
- desired entry zone;
- target or target logic;
- invalidation condition;
- proposed position size;
- maximum acceptable loss;
- confidence and risk rating.

## Shorts
Shorts are exceptional and speculative. A short can be proposed only when:
1. There is a concrete catalyst or highly specific downside scenario.
2. Entry, stop, target and time horizon are predefined.
3. Maximum loss is capped before entry.
4. Position size is smaller than a comparable core long unless explicitly approved otherwise.
5. The trade receives manual approval.

Short positions must be labeled `SPECULATIVE SHORT` and kept distinct from the core portfolio thesis.

## Signal Intelligence
Sources are scored by prediction quality, not by secrecy. A source can be observed for a long period without trading on its claims. Questionable non-public or unlawfully sourced information is observation-only until a lawful public basis for the trade exists.
